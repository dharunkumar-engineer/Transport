Frequently Asked Questions

 

How do I book a trailer with Chandrandeepa Transport?

Complete the form above with your route and cargo size, or call us on 96294 52208 or 90257 55248. We will reply with the suitable trailer and a rate.

How is the transport cost determined?

The rate depends on the distance between pick-up and delivery, the length, width, height and weight of your cargo, and the trailer required (60, 70 or 80 ft). Send us these details and we will provide a quotation.

How can I get an update on my consignment?

Call us on the numbers above at any time and we will inform you of the status of your goods.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve routes all over India, starting from Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.
