How do I book a trailer with Chandrandeepa Transport?

Fill in the booking form above with your route and cargo size, or call us on 96294 52208 or 90257 55248. We will reply with the right trailer and a rate.

How much does trailer transport cost?

The rate depends on the distance between pick-up and delivery, the length, width, height and weight of your cargo, and the trailer needed (60, 70 or 80 ft). Send us these details and we will quote you.

How do I get an update on my consignment?

Call us on the numbers above at any time and we will tell you where your goods are.
